# CityCare: key screens

The 15 screens that show how the system meets the clinic's requirements, plus 5 of them on a phone.
Captured from the demo data (every account's password is `password`).

## Desktop (1440 px)

| # | Screen | Requirement it shows | Signed in as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Home page](desktop/01-home-page.png) | Patients see free slots and can book | — |
| 2 | [Log in](desktop/02-log-in.png) | One system, access by role | — |
| 3 | [Admin dashboard](desktop/03-admin-dashboard.png) | Management overview: appointments, revenue, attendance | Administrator |
| 4 | [Reports](desktop/04-reports.png) | Daily lists, schedules, payments, patient visits as PDF / Excel / CSV | Administrator |
| 5 | [Front desk dashboard](desktop/05-front-desk-dashboard.png) | Shorter waits: today's queue, check-in, doctors on duty | Receptionist |
| 6 | [Book appointment](desktop/06-book-appointment-no-double-booking.png) | **No double-booking**: booked slots can't be picked | Receptionist |
| 7 | [Doctor schedules](desktop/07-doctor-schedules.png) | Weekly hours that generate the bookable slots | Receptionist |
| 8 | [Register patient](desktop/08-register-patient-duplicate-warning.png) | **No duplicate records**: same phone or name + birth date is flagged | Receptionist |
| 9 | [Patient record](desktop/09-patient-record-treatment-history.png) | **Doctors see previous visits**: treatment timeline, allergies | Doctor |
| 10 | [Doctor dashboard](desktop/10-doctor-dashboard.png) | The doctor's day and follow-ups | Doctor |
| 11 | [Consultation notes](desktop/11-consultation-notes.png) | Diagnosis, treatment, prescription; saving raises the invoice | Doctor |
| 12 | [Appointment detail](desktop/12-appointment-visit-progress.png) | Visit progress: booked → checked in → seen → invoiced → paid | Administrator |
| 13 | [Cash desk dashboard](desktop/13-cash-desk-dashboard.png) | **Cashiers see who has paid** and what is outstanding | Cashier |
| 14 | [Invoice and payment](desktop/14-invoice-and-payment.png) | Cash, MTN / Airtel Mobile Money, card or insurance | Cashier |
| 15 | [Patient portal](desktop/15-patient-portal.png) | Patients see their appointments, bills and visit history | Patient |
